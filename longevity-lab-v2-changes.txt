Longevity Lab v0.2 — Changes & Improvements
=============================================
Date: June 2026


NEW PANELS
----------

AI Narrative
  A full-paragraph clinical analysis generated via the Anthropic API on load.
  Only statistical summaries (no raw activity data) are sent.
  Appears as a dark editorial block at the top of the dashboard, before the KPIs.

Aerobic Efficiency Trend (§02)
  Pace-per-HR index plotted over time with a regression line.
  This is the closest proxy to VO₂max available from CSV data.
  A rising slope means the cardiovascular system is adapting;
  a falling slope is an early warning signal.
  Essential for CSV-only users who don't have a dedicated VO₂max series.

parkrun series (§03)
  Auto-detected from activity titles containing "parkrun".
  Scatter chart + rolling 4-week average line.
  PR highlighted, table of recent results included.
  Framed as a longitudinal biomarker rather than just race results.

Running form & economy (§06)
  Cadence + ground contact time dual-axis chart over time.
  Benchmarks applied: 170–180 spm optimal cadence, <260ms GCT target.
  Trend direction shown for cadence over the data window.
  Also pulls vertical oscillation and vertical ratio averages.

Training consistency heatmap (§07)
  GitHub-style calendar grid showing every day's activity type.
  Running = oxblood red, Strength = sage green, Rest/walk = light grey.
  Immediately reveals training consistency patterns and missed weeks.


FIXES & IMPROVEMENTS
---------------------

Monthly volume chart replaces yearly
  Much more useful for data windows shorter than 2 years.
  Strength session count overlaid on same chart (scaled for visibility).

Strength session frequency added as a KPI
  Shown with red/amber/green classification:
    >= 2/week = on target (green)
    >= 1/week = sub-optimal (amber)
    < 1/week  = insufficient (red)

KPI strip adapts when no VO₂ data is available
  Shows weekly running km average instead of VO₂max.

Intensity zone computation improved
  Now estimates max HR from observed activity data when no DOB is supplied,
  rather than silently skipping the zones panel.

All-time best efforts marked with ★ in the performance table
  Distinguishes personal bests from year bests at a glance.

Footer updated to v0.2


RECOMMENDATIONS ENGINE UPDATES
--------------------------------

Now incorporates aerobic efficiency trend alongside VO₂ decline rate.
Strength frequency explicitly flagged when below 2×/week.
parkrun series referenced in recommendations when enough data is present.
Intensity split recommendation uses actual zone percentages in copy.
